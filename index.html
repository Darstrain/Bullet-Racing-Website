BULLET RACING

Bullet Racing is an ambitious and driven team of 6 students @Windsor, England participating in the STEM Racing Competition.
Three seasons of design, enterprise, manufacturing and aerodynamic development, built around a single objective: maximum velocity over the 20-metre race.

COMPETITION

STEM Racing is an educational competition where teams of students design, build, test, and race miniature Formula 1-style cars. We employ cutting edge CAD and CFD Software to build and test 3d Models.
Throughout 3 years of competition thus far, we have developed strong cars for both Entry and Development classes, Winning Best Emerging Car Award. The competition also develops important skills such as problem-solving, communication, project management, and budgeting

ENTRY CLASS

Our first year of the tournament saw us learning the ropes and learning the basics of design and manufacturing. We developed a team, learnt key software and through rigorous trial and error, devised an aerodynamic and drag-minimising car.

Winning 4/14 overall and the Emerging Talent Award motivated us and enabled us to learn from the competition.

DEVELOPMENT CLASS 2025

Our second year of the tournament saw us taking our skills further in a vastly more competitive landscape. We utilised CNC modeling to reduce weight, demonstrated consistent project management and enterprise portfolios, developed a 40% faster car and came 7/16 amidst a challenging field.

We learnt aerodynamic theory, advanced simulation software and developed portfolios to demonstrate improved engineering skills.

DEVELOPMENT CLASS 2026

The car for this year's competition is currently under development. Relying on a breadth of competition experience and our strongest team yet, we are aiming for a podium finish and qualification to the National Round.

Keep tuned for updates and please consider sponsoring us to help us realise our ambitions!.
